\---



\## Project Overview



\*\*Project Name:\*\* MenuVision 3D



\*\*Project Description:\*\*



MenuVision 3D is a web-based interactive restaurant menu system where customers scan a QR code to view the restaurant's digital menu and explore dishes using interactive 3D models. Customers can rotate and zoom the dishes before deciding what to order. The actual order is still taken by the waiter, allowing restaurants to keep their existing workflow while improving the customer experience.



\---



\## Decisions Made



\- The first version will support \*\*one restaurant only\*\*.

\- Multi-restaurant support will be added in a future version.

\- Customers will \*\*not\*\* place orders through the application.

\- Waiters will continue taking orders manually.

\- The application's primary purpose is to provide an interactive 3D menu experience.
